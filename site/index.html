Get the app

Three steps to your first post.

Quota runs as a web app you add to your home screen. It installs in under a minute and behaves like any other app.

Install Quota

Open the web app in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen. Store listings are coming; the links will appear here.

Join or start a group

Open the invite link a friend sent you, or make a group and share yours. Two people is enough.

Set your quota

Name the goal, pick the days it applies to, and post your first proof before midnight.

Installing the web app.

iPhone

  1. Open the Quota link in Safari. It has to be Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Quota now opens from your home screen like any app.

Android

  1. Open the Quota link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see it.
  4. Tap Install. Quota appears with your other apps.

Notifications and the camera work once the app is installed. If you skip the install and use it in the browser, reminders will not arrive.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Posting

What counts as proof?

A photo or a clip of up to ten seconds, taken with Quota's camera that day. Quota checks when it was taken, so something old from your camera roll won't go through.

Why can't I upload something I already took?

Because then anyone could. The point of Quota is that the proof was made today. The in-app camera is the only way in.

Do I have to post every single day?

No. Each quota has active days. Days outside them leave your streak alone.

What if I'm travelling in another time zone?

Midnight is midnight where your phone is. Your deadline moves with you.

Streaks and groups

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets, and so does the group's. A group day only counts when every member posts.

What if someone posts something that clearly doesn't count?

Anyone in the group can flag it, and the group votes. Quota doesn't decide, and neither do we.

Can I be in more than one group?

Yes. Each group has its own streak and you can have a different quota in each.

Can I use it on my own?

You can, but on your own it's a diary with a camera. Quota is a group thing. Make a group, or open the link somebody sent you.

Can I leave a group?

Yes, any time, from the group's settings. Your past posts stay in the group. The group's streak carries on without you.

Account

Do I need an email to sign up?

No. You pick a username and a password. There's no email address on your account at all.

What if I forget my password?

When you sign up, Quota gives you recovery codes. Save them somewhere safe. Each one resets your password once, and since there's no email, they're the way back in.

How do friends find me?

By your username. Or skip that and send them your group's invite link.

Privacy and cost

Who can see my posts?

Your group. There is no public feed, no explore page, no strangers.

How much does it cost?

The base version is free and always will be. A paid version with extra perks will come eventually.

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. Today Quota runs as a web app you add to your home screen. Store links will appear on the Get the app page when they're ready.

How do I delete my account?

Email us and ask. Your account and everything in it is deleted within 30 days. You can also ask for a copy of everything we hold first.

Does Quota show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no cookies. Nothing is sold.

How do I get in touch?

Email us. The address is in the footer. You'll hear back from one of the two of us.
